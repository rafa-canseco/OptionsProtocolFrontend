A. Tabs above the trading panel

Horizontal tab bar at the top of /earn. One click to switch. All assets visible at once. Scales with scrollable tabs.

Pros

  • Zero navigation, instant switch
  • Always see what's available

Cons

  • Gets cramped at 8+ assets
  • No room for asset details (price, IV)

B. Asset sidebar / left rail

Vertical list of assets on the left. Main trading panel stays to the right. Each asset can show price/IV inline. Scales infinitely by scrolling.

Pros

  • Scales to many assets
  • Room for price/change next to each

Cons

  • Eats horizontal space
  • 3-column layout on mobile is tight

C. Dropdown selector above the panel

Compact dropdown showing current asset + live price. Opens a searchable list on click. Minimal footprint, all space goes to trading.

Pros

  • Maximum space for trading UI
  • Scales infinitely (searchable list)
  • Room for price/IV next to selector

Cons

  • Hides available assets (extra click)
  • Less discoverable for new users

D. Dedicated asset selection page

A /markets or landing page showing all assets as cards with price/IV. Click one to go to /earn/[asset]. URL-driven routing.

Pros

  • Rich info per asset (price, IV, volume)
  • Clean separation of concerns
  • Great for 10+ assets

Cons

  • Extra navigation step to switch
  • More pages to build and maintain
